🧩 Sentence Builder

Learn Chinese through reusable sentence patterns.

Basic Sentence

Subject + Verb + Object

我学习中文。

Wǒ xuéxí Zhōngwén.

I study Chinese.

Yes/No Question

Statement + 吗？

你学习中文吗？

Nǐ xuéxí Zhōngwén ma?

Do you study Chinese?

Negation

Subject + 不 + Verb

我不喝咖啡。

Wǒ bù hē kāfēi.

I don't drink coffee.

Possession

Person + 的 + Noun

我的书。

Wǒ de shū.

My book.

💬 Real Conversation

Practice a complete beginner conversation.

A
你好！你叫什么名字？
Nǐ hǎo! Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?

Hello! What is your name?

B
你好！我叫李明。
Nǐ hǎo! Wǒ jiào Lǐ Míng.

Hello! My name is Li Ming.

A
你学习中文吗？
Nǐ xuéxí Zhōngwén ma?

Do you study Chinese?

B
是的，我学习中文。
Shì de, wǒ xuéxí Zhōngwén.

Yes, I study Chinese.

📖 Reading Practice

Read a short beginner-level Chinese passage.

我叫王明。我是学生。 我每天学习中文，也喜欢看书。 星期六，我和朋友一起去公园。
Wǒ jiào Wáng Míng. Wǒ shì xuésheng. Wǒ měitiān xuéxí Zhōngwén, yě xǐhuan kàn shū. Xīngqīliù, wǒ hé péngyou yìqǐ qù gōngyuán.

My name is Wang Ming. I am a student. I study Chinese every day and also like reading. On Saturday, I go to the park with my friends.
