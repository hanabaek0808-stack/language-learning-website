📚 B1 Vocabulary

Build your intermediate vocabulary with meanings, Urdu translations, examples and pronunciation.

💭 1. Opinions & Ideas

Opinion

/əˈpɪnjən/

Urdu: رائے

Meaning: What you think about something

Example: In my opinion, this book is useful.

Suggest

/səˈdʒest/

Urdu: تجویز دینا

Meaning: To give an idea or plan

Example: I suggest taking a short break.

Reason

/ˈriːzən/

Urdu: وجہ

Meaning: The cause of something

Example: What is the reason for the delay?

Decision

/dɪˈsɪʒən/

Urdu: فیصلہ

Meaning: A choice made after thinking

Example: It was a difficult decision.

Believe

/bɪˈliːv/

Urdu: یقین کرنا

Meaning: To think something is true

Example: I believe that education is important.

💼 2. Career & Work

Experience

/ɪkˈspɪəriəns/

Urdu: تجربہ

Meaning: Knowledge gained by doing something

Example: She has teaching experience.

Opportunity

/ˌɑːpərˈtuːnəti/

Urdu: موقع

Meaning: A chance to do something

Example: This is a great opportunity.

Skill

/skɪl/

Urdu: مہارت

Meaning: An ability to do something well

Example: Communication is an important skill.

Improve

/ɪmˈpruːv/

Urdu: بہتر کرنا

Meaning: To make something better

Example: I want to improve my English.

Career

/kəˈrɪr/

Urdu: پیشہ، کیریئر

Meaning: The work you do over a period of your life

Example: She wants a career in education.

🌍 3. Society & Environment

Environment

/ɪnˈvaɪrənmənt/

Urdu: ماحول

Meaning: The natural world around us

Example: We should protect the environment.

Pollution

/pəˈluːʃən/

Urdu: آلودگی

Meaning: Harmful substances in the environment

Example: Air pollution is a serious problem.

Community

/kəˈmjuːnəti/

Urdu: برادری، کمیونٹی

Meaning: People living in the same area or sharing interests

Example: Our community needs a better library.

Public

/ˈpʌblɪk/

Urdu: عوامی

Meaning: Relating to ordinary people or society

Example: This is a public park.

Protect

/prəˈtekt/

Urdu: حفاظت کرنا

Meaning: To keep someone or something safe

Example: We must protect our forests.

Knowledge

/ˈnɑːlɪdʒ/

Urdu: علم

Meaning: Information and understanding

Example: Reading increases knowledge.

Research

/rɪˈsɜːrtʃ/

Urdu: تحقیق

Meaning: Careful study to discover information

Example: She is doing research for her project.

Develop

/dɪˈveləp/

Urdu: ترقی دینا، نشوونما دینا

Meaning: To grow or become better

Example: Reading helps develop language skills.

Understand

/ˌʌndərˈstænd/

Urdu: سمجھنا

Meaning: To know the meaning of something

Example: I understand the question.

Practice

/ˈpræktɪs/

Urdu: مشق

Meaning: Repeated activity to improve a skill

Example: Practice helps you improve.

Respect

Save

/seɪv/

Example: I am saving money for a new phone.

Spend

/spend/

Urdu: خرچ کرنا

Example: Don't spend all your money.

Cost

/kɔːst/

Urdu: قیمت ہونا

Meaning: The amount of money needed for something

Example: How much does it cost?

Profit

/ˈprɑːfɪt/
