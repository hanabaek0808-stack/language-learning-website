🔊 Arabic Harakat

Learn the short vowel marks used to guide pronunciation.

بَ

Fatha

Usually represents a short "a" sound.

بِ

Kasra

Usually represents a short "i" sound.

بُ

Damma

Usually represents a short "u" sound.

بْ

Sukūn

Shows that the consonant has no short vowel.

بّ

Shadda

Indicates a doubled consonant.

بً

Tanwin

Used with certain indefinite noun endings.

🌱 Arabic Root System

Discover how related words can be built from common roots.

Example: ك ت ب

The root ك ت ب is associated with writing and books. Arabic vocabulary often develops through patterns built around roots.

كَتَبَ كِتَاب كَاتِب مَكْتَب مَكْتَبَة

Writing → Book → Writer → Office/Desk → Library

✏️ Arabic Grammar Foundations

Build grammar from simple patterns before moving to advanced structures.

1. Gender

Arabic nouns are commonly masculine or feminine. Many feminine nouns end in ة.

طَالِبٌ — طَالِبَةٌ

Student — male / female

2. Definite Article

The prefix ال can make a noun definite.

كِتَابٌ → الْكِتَابُ

A book → The book

3. Demonstratives

Use هَذَا for "this" with a masculine singular noun and هَذِهِ with a feminine singular noun.

هَذَا كِتَابٌ. — هَذِهِ مَدْرَسَةٌ.

This is a book. — This is a school.

4. Basic Verbal Sentence

A common Arabic verbal sentence can begin with a verb followed by the subject.

ذَهَبَ الطَّالِبُ إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ.

The student went to school.

5. Possession

Arabic commonly expresses possession through an iḍāfah construction.

كِتَابُ الطَّالِبِ

The student's book.

💬 Everyday Arabic Communication

Learn expressions you can actually use in conversations.

Greeting

السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ
As-salāmu ʿalaykum
Peace be upon you / Hello

Asking about someone

كَيْفَ حَالُكَ؟
Kayfa ḥāluka?
How are you?

Introducing yourself

اِسْمِي أَحْمَدُ.
Ismī Aḥmad.
My name is Ahmad.

Asking for help

هَلْ يُمْكِنُكَ مُسَاعَدَتِي؟
Hal yumkinuka musāʿadatī?
Can you help me?

When you don't understand

لَا أَفْهَمُ.
Lā afhamu.
I don't understand.

Asking someone to repeat

مِنْ فَضْلِكَ، أَعِدْ ذَلِكَ.
Min faḍlika, aʿid dhālika.
Please repeat that.

📖 Beginner Arabic Reading

Read a short passage and understand it in context.

أَنَا طَالِبٌ. أَذْهَبُ إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ كُلَّ يَوْمٍ. أُحِبُّ قِرَاءَةَ الْكُتُبِ وَتَعَلُّمَ اللُّغَةِ الْعَرَبِيَّةِ.
I am a student. I go to school every day. I like reading books and learning Arabic.
