✏️ Basic German Grammar

Learn important German grammar patterns.

Present Tense

The present tense is used for actions happening now or things that happen regularly.

Example: Ich lerne Deutsch.


Past Tense

The past tense is used for completed actions.

Example: Ich habe Deutsch gelernt.


Future Tense

The future tense is used for actions that will happen.

Example: Ich werde morgen Deutsch lernen.

💬 Everyday German

Useful expressions for daily communication.

Hallo!

Meaning: Hello!

Example: Hallo! Wie geht es dir?

Wie geht es dir?

Meaning: How are you?

Example: Hallo, wie geht es dir?

Vielen Dank.

Meaning: Thank you very much.

Example: Vielen Dank für deine Hilfe.

Kannst du mir helfen?

Meaning: Can you help me?

Example: Kannst du mir bitte helfen?
