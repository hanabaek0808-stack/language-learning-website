🧠 1. Critical Thinking

Scrutinise

/ˈskruːtənaɪz/

Urdu: باریک بینی سے جانچنا

Meaning: To examine something extremely carefully

Example: The researchers scrutinised the evidence.

Infer

/ɪnˈfɜːr/

Urdu: اخذ کرنا

Meaning: To reach a conclusion from evidence

Example: We can infer several conclusions from the data.

Coherent

/koʊˈhɪrənt/

Urdu: مربوط، منطقی

Meaning: Logical and well organised

Example: She presented a coherent argument.

Ambiguous

/æmˈbɪɡjuəs/

Urdu: مبہم

Meaning: Having more than one possible meaning

Example: The statement was deliberately ambiguous.

Substantiate

/səbˈstænʃieɪt/

Urdu: ثبوت سے ثابت کرنا

Meaning: To provide evidence that proves something

Example: The claim could not be substantiated.

📚 2. Academic & Literary English

Paradigm

/ˈpærədaɪm/

Urdu: نمونہ، نظریاتی ڈھانچہ

Meaning: A typical model or pattern

Example: The discovery changed the scientific paradigm.

Nuance

/ˈnuːɑːns/

Urdu: باریک فرق

Meaning: A subtle difference in meaning or expression

Example: The novel contains many cultural nuances.

Empirical

/ɪmˈpɪrɪkəl/

Urdu: تجرباتی

Meaning: Based on observation or experiment

Example: The theory requires empirical evidence.

Connotation

/ˌkɑːnəˈteɪʃən/

Urdu: ضمنی مفہوم

Meaning: An additional meaning associated with a word

Example: The word has a negative connotation.

Rhetorical

/rɪˈtɔːrɪkəl/

Urdu: خطیبانہ

Meaning: Related to effective or persuasive language

Example: The speaker used a powerful rhetorical question.

💼 3. Professional English

Leverage

/ˈlevərɪdʒ/

Urdu: مؤثر طور پر استعمال کرنا

Meaning: To use something to maximum advantage

Example: We can leverage technology to improve efficiency.

Mitigate

/ˈmɪtɪɡeɪt/

Urdu: کم کرنا

Meaning: To reduce the seriousness of something

Example: The policy aims to mitigate the risks.

Facilitate

/fəˈsɪlɪteɪt/

Urdu: آسان بنانا

Meaning: To make an action or process easier

Example: The meeting facilitated cooperation.

Allocate

/ˈæləkeɪt/

Urdu: مختص کرنا

Meaning: To distribute something for a particular purpose

Example: The organisation allocated more money to education.

Accountability

/əˌkaʊntəˈbɪləti/

Urdu: جواب دہی

Meaning: Responsibility for actions and decisions

Example: Public officials should have accountability.

🌍 4. Society & Global Issues

Disparity

/dɪˈspærəti/

Urdu: تفاوت

Meaning: A significant difference between things

Example: There is a large disparity in income.

Marginalised

/ˈmɑːrdʒɪnəlaɪzd/

Urdu: حاشیے پر دھکیلا ہوا

Meaning: Treated as less important or excluded

Example: The programme supports marginalised communities.

Prejudice

/ˈpredʒədɪs/

Urdu: تعصب

Meaning: An unfair opinion formed without enough knowledge

Example: Education can help reduce prejudice.

Sovereignty

/ˈsɑːvrənti/

Urdu: خود مختاری

Meaning: The authority of a country to govern itself

Example: The country defended its sovereignty.

Humanitarian

/hjuːˌmænɪˈteriən/

Urdu: انسانی ہمدردی سے متعلق

Meaning: Concerned with improving human welfare

Example: The organisation provided humanitarian assistance.

🌱 5. Environment & Science

Ecological

/ˌiːkəˈlɑːdʒɪkəl/

Urdu: ماحولیاتی

Meaning: Related to the natural environment

Example: The project has serious ecological consequences.

Contamination

/kənˌtæməˈneɪʃən/

Urdu: آلودگی

Meaning: The process of making something impure or polluted

Example: Water contamination is a serious problem.

Biodiversity

/ˌbaɪoʊdaɪˈvɜːrsəti/

Urdu: حیاتیاتی تنوع

Meaning: The variety of living things in an environment

Example: Forests contain enormous biodiversity.

Sustainable

/səˈsteɪnəbəl/

Urdu: پائیدار

Meaning: Able to continue without damaging resources

Example: We need sustainable development.

Degradation

/ˌdeɡrəˈdeɪʃən/

Urdu: تنزلی، خرابی

Meaning: The process of becoming worse

Example: Soil degradation threatens agriculture.

🗣️ 6. Sophisticated Communication

Eloquent

/ˈeləkwənt/

Urdu: فصیح و بلیغ

Meaning: Fluent or persuasive in speaking or writing

Example: She gave an eloquent speech.

Concise

/kənˈsaɪs/

Urdu: مختصر اور جامع

Meaning: Expressing much in few words

Example: Please keep your answer concise.

Diplomatic

/ˌdɪpləˈmætɪk/

Urdu: سفارتی، محتاط

Meaning: Careful not to offend people

Example: He gave a diplomatic response.

Articulate

/ɑːrˈtɪkjəleɪt/

Urdu: واضح انداز میں بیان کرنا

Meaning: To express ideas clearly and effectively

Example: He is able to articulate complex ideas.

Provoke

/prəˈvoʊk/

Urdu: اکسایا، ابھارنا

Meaning: To cause a reaction or feeling

Example: The article provoked a strong response.

⚖️ 7. Argument & Debate

Concede

/kənˈsiːd/

Urdu: تسلیم کرنا

Meaning: To admit that something is true

Example: He conceded that his opponent had a valid point.

Refute

/rɪˈfjuːt/

Urdu: تردید کرنا

Meaning: To prove that an argument is wrong

Example: The evidence refuted the original claim.

Counterargument

/ˈkaʊntərˌɑːrɡjəmənt/

Urdu: جوابی دلیل

Meaning: An argument against another argument

Example: She presented a strong counterargument.

Contention

/kənˈtenʃən/

Urdu: دعویٰ، مؤقف

Meaning: An argument or claim

Example: His central contention was convincing.

Premise

/ˈpremɪs/

Urdu: بنیادی مفروضہ

Meaning: An idea used as the basis of an argument

Example: The argument is based on a questionable premise.

Ubiquitous

/juːˈbɪkwɪtəs/

Foster

Comprehend

Anticipate
