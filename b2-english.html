Approach

📚 B2 Vocabulary

Develop a stronger vocabulary with useful B2 words, Urdu translations, meanings, examples and pronunciation.

🌍 1. Society

Society

/səˈsaɪəti/

Urdu: معاشرہ

Meaning: People living together as a community

Example: Education is important for society.

Equality

/ɪˈkwɑːləti/

Urdu: مساوات

Meaning: The state of being equal

Example: Everyone deserves equality.

Government

/ˈɡʌvərnmənt/

Urdu: حکومت

Meaning: The group that controls a country

Example: The government introduced a new policy.

Citizen

/ˈsɪtɪzən/

Urdu: شہری

Meaning: A legally recognized member of a country

Example: Every citizen has rights and responsibilities.

Responsibility

/rɪˌspɑːnsəˈbɪləti/

Urdu: ذمہ داری

Meaning: Something that you are expected to do

Example: Helping others is a social responsibility.

🌱 2. Environment

Sustainable

/səˈsteɪnəbəl/

Urdu: پائیدار

Meaning: Able to continue without causing serious harm

Example: We need sustainable sources of energy.

Climate

/ˈklaɪmət/

Urdu: آب و ہوا

Meaning: The usual weather of a place

Example: Climate change affects many countries.

Conservation

/ˌkɑːnsərˈveɪʃən/

Urdu: تحفظ

Meaning: Protection of nature and resources

Example: Wildlife conservation is essential.

Resource

/ˈriːsɔːrs/

Urdu: وسیلہ، ذریعہ

Meaning: Something useful that can be used

Example: Water is an important natural resource.

Waste

/weɪst/

Urdu: فضلہ

Meaning: Unwanted material

Example: We should reduce food waste.

🎓 3. Education

Academic

/ˌækəˈdemɪk/

Urdu: تعلیمی

Meaning: Related to education or study

Example: She has excellent academic results.

Curriculum

/kəˈrɪkjələm/

Urdu: نصاب

Meaning: The subjects taught in a course

Example: The school changed its curriculum.

Assignment

/əˈsaɪnmənt/

Urdu: تعلیمی کام

Meaning: A piece of work given to students

Example: I finished my assignment.

Researcher

/rɪˈsɜːrtʃər/

Urdu: محقق

Meaning: A person who carries out research

Example: The researcher collected new information.

Evaluate

/ɪˈvæljueɪt/

Urdu: جانچنا، جائزہ لینا

Meaning: To judge the quality or value of something

Example: Teachers evaluate students' work.

💻 4. Technology

Device

/dɪˈvaɪs/

Urdu: آلہ

Meaning: A piece of equipment designed for a purpose

Example: This device is easy to use.

Digital

/ˈdɪdʒɪtəl/

Urdu: ڈیجیٹل

Meaning: Using electronic technology

Example: Many people use digital services.

Artificial

/ˌɑːrtɪˈfɪʃəl/

Urdu: مصنوعی

Meaning: Made by humans rather than naturally

Example: Artificial intelligence is developing quickly.

Privacy

/ˈpraɪvəsi/

Urdu: رازداری

Meaning: The right to keep personal information private

Example: Online privacy is important.

Access

/ˈækses/

Urdu: رسائی

Meaning: The ability to use or reach something

Example: Students have access to the internet.

📈 5. Business & Economy

Investment

/ɪnˈvestmənt/

Urdu: سرمایہ کاری

Meaning: Money put into something to gain a benefit

Example: Education is a valuable investment.

Income

/ˈɪnkʌm/

Urdu: آمدنی

Meaning: Money received regularly

Example: His monthly income has increased.

Consumer

/kənˈsuːmər/

Urdu: صارف

Meaning: A person who buys or uses goods and services

Example: Consumers compare prices before buying.

Demand

/dɪˈmænd/

Urdu: طلب

Meaning: The desire or need for something

Example: There is high demand for skilled workers.

Affordable

/əˈfɔːrdəbəl/

Urdu: قابلِ استطاعت

Meaning: Not too expensive

Example: The company offers affordable products.

🗣️ 6. Communication

Communicate

/kəˈmjuːnɪkeɪt/

Urdu: رابطہ کرنا

Meaning: To share information or ideas

Example: Good leaders communicate clearly.

Persuade

/pərˈsweɪd/

Urdu: قائل کرنا

Meaning: To convince someone to do something

Example: She persuaded me to join the course.

Argument

/ˈɑːrɡjəmənt/

Urdu: دلیل، بحث

Meaning: A reason given to support an idea

Example: He presented a strong argument.

Discuss

/dɪˈskʌs/

Urdu: بحث کرنا

Meaning: To talk about something in detail

Example: We need to discuss this problem.

Express

/ɪkˈspres/

Urdu: اظہار کرنا

Meaning: To show or communicate an idea or feeling

Example: She expressed her opinion clearly.

Example: We should eat a balanced diet.

Exercise

Example: Regular exercise is good for you.

Prevent

/prɪˈvent/

Meaning: To stop something from happening

Example: Exercise can help prevent some health problems.

Treatment

/ˈtriːtmənt/

Example: The patient received treatment.

Stress

Example: Too much work can cause stress.

Example: Paris is a popular destination.

Culture

/ˈkʌltʃər/

Meaning: The customs and beliefs of a group

Example: Every country has its own culture.

Traditional

/trəˈdɪʃənəl/

Example: We wore traditional clothes.

Explore

/ɪkˈsplɔːr/

Urdu: دریافت کرنا، گھومنا

Accommodation

/əˌkɑːməˈdeɪʃən/

/ˈtʃælɪndʒ/

Example: Learning a language can be a challenge.

Solution

Meaning: An answer to a problem

Example: We need to find a solution.

Cause

/kɔːz/

Meaning: The reason something happens

Example: We need to find the cause of the problem.

Effect

/ɪˈfekt/

Reduce

Require

Example: This job requires experience.

Approach

/əˈproʊtʃ/

Example: We need a different approach.

Efficient

/ɪˈfɪʃənt/

Urdu: مؤثر

Example: This is an efficient way to study.

Impact

Example: Technology has a major impact on education.
