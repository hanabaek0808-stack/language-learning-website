📚 C1 Vocabulary

Develop advanced vocabulary with Urdu meanings, clear explanations, examples and pronunciation.

🧠 1. Ideas & Critical Thinking

Analyse

/ˈænəlaɪz/

Urdu: تجزیہ کرنا

Meaning: To examine something carefully

Example: Students should analyse the evidence carefully.

Assess

/əˈses/

Urdu: جائزہ لینا

Meaning: To judge the quality or importance of something

Example: The teacher assessed the students' progress.

Interpret

/ɪnˈtɜːrprət/

Urdu: تشریح کرنا

Meaning: To explain the meaning of something

Example: It is difficult to interpret the results.

Perspective

/pərˈspektɪv/

Urdu: نقطۂ نظر

Meaning: A particular way of viewing something

Example: We need to consider the issue from another perspective.

Assumption

/əˈsʌmpʃən/

Urdu: مفروضہ

Meaning: Something accepted as true without proof

Example: His argument was based on a false assumption.

🎓 2. Academic English

Concept

/ˈkɑːnsept/

Urdu: تصور

Meaning: An idea or principle

Example: The concept is difficult to understand.

Hypothesis

/haɪˈpɑːθəsɪs/

Urdu: مفروضہ

Meaning: An idea that can be tested

Example: The researchers tested their hypothesis.

Evidence

/ˈevɪdəns/

Urdu: ثبوت

Meaning: Information that supports an idea

Example: There is strong evidence for this theory.

Significant

/sɪɡˈnɪfɪkənt/

Urdu: اہم، نمایاں

Meaning: Important or noticeable

Example: The study produced significant results.

Furthermore

/ˌfɜːrðərˈmɔːr/

Urdu: مزید برآں

Meaning: In addition to what has already been said

Example: Furthermore, the results support our argument.

💼 3. Professional English

Negotiate

/nɪˈɡoʊʃieɪt/

Urdu: مذاکرات کرنا

Meaning: To discuss something to reach an agreement

Example: They negotiated a new contract.

Implement

/ˈɪmplɪment/

Urdu: نافذ کرنا

Meaning: To put a plan into action

Example: The company implemented a new policy.

Prioritise

/praɪˈɔːrətaɪz/

Urdu: ترجیح دینا

Meaning: To decide what is most important

Example: We need to prioritise our main objectives.

Collaborate

/kəˈlæbəreɪt/

Urdu: تعاون کرنا

Meaning: To work together

Example: The two teams collaborated on the project.

Strategy

/ˈstrætədʒi/

Urdu: حکمت عملی

Meaning: A carefully planned method

Example: We need an effective strategy.

🌍 4. Society & Politics

Democracy

/dɪˈmɑːkrəsi/

Urdu: جمہوریت

Meaning: A system in which people choose their representatives

Example: Democracy depends on informed citizens.

Legislation

/ˌledʒɪˈsleɪʃən/

Urdu: قانون سازی

Meaning: Laws made by a government

Example: New legislation was introduced.

Controversial

/ˌkɑːntrəˈvɜːrʃəl/

Urdu: متنازع

Meaning: Causing disagreement or argument

Example: The decision was highly controversial.

Inequality

/ˌɪnɪˈkwɑːləti/

Urdu: عدم مساوات

Meaning: A lack of equality

Example: The policy aims to reduce inequality.

Reform

/rɪˈfɔːrm/

Urdu: اصلاحات

Meaning: A change made to improve a system

Example: The government introduced educational reforms.

🌱 5. Environment & Science

Depletion

/dɪˈpliːʃən/

Urdu: کمی، ختم ہونا

Meaning: A reduction in the amount of something

Example: The depletion of natural resources is concerning.

Emission

/ɪˈmɪʃən/

Urdu: اخراج

Meaning: The release of gases or substances

Example: Governments want to reduce carbon emissions.

Ecosystem

/ˈiːkoʊsɪstəm/

Urdu: ماحولیاتی نظام

Meaning: A community of living things and their environment

Example: Pollution can damage an ecosystem.

Innovation

/ˌɪnəˈveɪʃən/

Urdu: جدت

Meaning: A new idea or method

Example: Innovation can improve people's lives.

Phenomenon

/fəˈnɑːmənən/

Urdu: مظہر، واقعہ

Meaning: Something that can be observed or experienced

Example: Climate change is a global phenomenon.

🗣️ 6. Communication

Articulate

/ɑːrˈtɪkjəleɪt/

Urdu: واضح انداز میں بیان کرنا

Meaning: To express ideas clearly

Example: She articulated her views clearly.

Clarify

/ˈklærəfaɪ/

Urdu: واضح کرنا

Meaning: To make something easier to understand

Example: Could you clarify your point?

Emphasise

/ˈemfəsaɪz/

Urdu: زور دینا

Meaning: To give special importance to something

Example: The teacher emphasised the importance of practice.

Convey

/kənˈveɪ/

Urdu: پہنچانا، اظہار کرنا

Meaning: To communicate an idea or feeling

Example: Words cannot always convey our emotions.

Misunderstanding

/ˌmɪsʌndərˈstændɪŋ/

Urdu: غلط فہمی

Meaning: A failure to understand correctly

Example: The disagreement resulted from a misunderstanding.

📊 7. Change & Development

Transformation

/ˌtrænsfərˈmeɪʃən/

Urdu: تبدیلی

Meaning: A major change in form or appearance

Example: Technology has caused a major transformation.

Decline

/dɪˈklaɪn/

Urdu: زوال، کمی

Meaning: A gradual decrease

Example: The industry experienced a decline.

Expand

/ɪkˈspænd/

Urdu: پھیلانا

Meaning: To become larger or more extensive

Example: The company plans to expand internationally.

Adapt

/əˈdæpt/

Urdu: مطابقت پیدا کرنا

Meaning: To change in order to deal with a new situation

Example: People must adapt to technological changes.

Accelerate

/əkˈseləreɪt/

Urdu: تیز کرنا

Meaning: To make something happen faster

Example: Technology has accelerated communication.

⚖️ 8. Arguments & Discussion

Nevertheless

/ˌnevərðəˈles/

Urdu: اس کے باوجود

Meaning: Despite what has just been said

Example: The task was difficult; nevertheless, we completed it.

Whereas

/werˈæz/

Urdu: جبکہ

Meaning: Used to compare two different facts

Example: Some people prefer cities, whereas others prefer villages.

Justify

/ˈdʒʌstɪfaɪ/

Urdu: جواز پیش کرنا

Meaning: To give a good reason for something

Example: Can you justify your decision?

Contradict

/ˌkɑːntrəˈdɪkt/

Urdu: تردید کرنا

Meaning: To say that something is not true

Example: His evidence contradicts the earlier report.

Compromise

/ˈkɑːmprəmaɪz/

Urdu: سمجھوتہ

Meaning: An agreement in which both sides accept less

Example: They reached a compromise.

✨ 9. Advanced Adjectives

Remarkable

/rɪˈmɑːrkəbəl/

Urdu: قابلِ ذکر

Meaning: Unusual or impressive

Example: She made remarkable progress.

Complex

/ˈkɑːmpleks/

Urdu: پیچیدہ

Meaning: Consisting of many connected parts

Example: This is a complex problem.

Essential

/ɪˈsenʃəl/

Urdu: ضروری

Meaning: Absolutely necessary

Example: Practice is essential for improvement.

Considerable

/kənˈsɪdərəbəl/

Urdu: قابلِ ذکر، خاصا

Meaning: Large or important enough to notice

Example: The project required considerable effort.

Contemporary

/kənˈtempəreri/

Urdu: موجودہ دور کا

Meaning: Belonging to the present time

Example: She studies contemporary literature.

Establish

/ɪˈstæblɪʃ/

Example: The study established a clear connection.

Generate

/ˈdʒenəreɪt/

Example: The project generated new ideas.

Facilitate

/fəˈsɪlɪteɪt/

Urdu: آسان بنانا

Meaning: To make something easier

Example: Technology can facilitate learning.
