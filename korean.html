✏️ Korean Grammar

Learn important Korean sentence structures.

1️⃣ Korean Word Order

Korean commonly follows a Subject–Object–Verb pattern.

Subject + Object + Verb

저는 한국어를 공부해요.

I study Korean.

2️⃣ 이에요 / 예요

Used to say that something is or identify something.

Noun + 이에요 / 예요

저는 학생이에요.

I am a student.

3️⃣ 은 / 는

Topic particles used to introduce or emphasize a topic.

Noun + 은 / 는

저는 학생이에요.

As for me, I am a student.

4️⃣ 을 / 를

Object particles mark the object of an action.

Object + 을 / 를

한국어를 공부해요.

I study Korean.

🗣️ Real-Life Korean Conversation

Practice a complete beginner conversation.

A:
안녕하세요!
Hello!
B:
안녕하세요. 잘 지냈어요?
Hello. Have you been well?
A:
네, 잘 지냈어요. 감사합니다.
Yes, I have been well. Thank you.
B:
만나서 반가워요.
Nice to meet you.
