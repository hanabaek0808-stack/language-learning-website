🐶 A New Friend

Ali sees a small dog in the park and decides to help it.

Ali went to the park one morning. He saw a small dog sitting under a tree. The dog looked tired and hungry. Ali gave the dog some water and food. After a while, the dog became happy. Ali smiled and took the dog home. They became good friends.

A2

📚 The First Book

Sara discovers a book that changes the way she sees learning.

Sara did not like reading very much. One day, her teacher gave her an interesting book. Sara started reading it before going to bed. The story was exciting, and she wanted to know what happened next. Soon, reading became her favorite habit.

B1

🏔️ The Mountain Journey

A young traveler begins an exciting journey into the mountains.

Daniel had always dreamed of visiting the mountains. One summer, he packed his bag and started his journey. The path was difficult, but he continued. After several hours, he reached the top. The view was beautiful. Daniel realized that difficult journeys can create wonderful memories.

B2

🌧️ The Unexpected Meeting

Two old friends meet again after many years.

It was raining heavily when Ahmed entered a small café. He looked around and suddenly recognized someone sitting near the window. It was his childhood friend, whom he had not seen for ten years. They spent the afternoon talking about their memories and dreams. Sometimes, life brings people together when they least expect it.
