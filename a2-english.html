✈️ 1. Travel

Journey

/ˈdʒɜːrni/

Urdu: سفر

Meaning: Travel from one place to another

Example: Our journey was very long.

Ticket

/ˈtɪkɪt/

Urdu: ٹکٹ

Meaning: A document that allows you to travel or enter a place

Example: I bought a train ticket.

Airport

/ˈerˌpɔːrt/

Urdu: ہوائی اڈہ

Meaning: A place where airplanes arrive and leave

Example: We arrived at the airport early.

Hotel

/hoʊˈtel/

Urdu: ہوٹل

Meaning: A place where people pay to stay

Example: We stayed in a small hotel.

Passport

/ˈpæspɔːrt/

Urdu: پاسپورٹ

Meaning: An official document used for international travel

Example: Don't forget your passport.
