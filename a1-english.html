👋 1. Greetings & Basic Words

Hello

/həˈloʊ/

Urdu: ہیلو، سلام

Meaning: A greeting

Example: Hello, how are you?

Goodbye

/ˌɡʊdˈbaɪ/

Urdu: خدا حافظ

Meaning: A way of saying farewell

Example: Goodbye, see you tomorrow.

Please

/pliːz/

Urdu: براہ کرم

Meaning: A polite word used when asking

Example: Please help me.

Thanks

/θæŋks/

Urdu: شکریہ

Meaning: A word used to show gratitude

Example: Thanks for your help.

Sorry

/ˈsɑːri/

Urdu: معذرت

Meaning: Used when apologizing

Example: I am sorry.
