📚 Common English Vocabulary

Start with useful everyday words.

Improve

Meaning

To make something better.

Example: I want to improve my English.

Opportunity

Meaning

A good chance to do something.

Example: Education gives us many opportunities.

Confident

Meaning

Feeling sure about your abilities.

Example: She feels confident when speaking English.

Important

Meaning

Having great value or significance.

Example: Learning vocabulary is important.

✏️ Basic Grammar

Learn some important English grammar patterns.

Present Simple

We use the present simple for habits, facts and regular actions.

Example: I study English every day.


Past Simple

We use the past simple for completed actions in the past.

Example: I studied English yesterday.


Future Simple

We use the future simple for actions that will happen in the future.

Example: I will study English tomorrow.

💬 Everyday English

Useful expressions for daily communication.

Hello!

Used when greeting someone.

Example: Hello! How are you?

How are you?

Used to ask someone about their well-being.

Example: How are you today?

Thank you.

Used to express gratitude.

Example: Thank you for your help.

Could you help me?

A polite way to ask for help.

Example: Could you help me with this?
